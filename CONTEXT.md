# Launch Party Games

This context covers the SeasonApproved launch-party competition and the language shown to participants and game masters.

## Language

**Participant**:
A named launch-party guest who competes as a member of exactly one Season Team.
_Avoid_: User, account, player

**Season Team**:
One of Winter, Spring, Summer, or Autumn; its score is the sum of its participants' awarded points.
_Avoid_: Group, squad

**Submission**:
A photo or screenshot a participant sends as evidence for a Scavenger Hunt item.
_Avoid_: Upload, result

**Game Master**:
The SeasonApproved analyst who reviews submissions and awards points.
_Avoid_: Admin, moderator

**Score Event**:
An awarded point contribution tied to a participant and an approved submission.
_Avoid_: Score row, adjustment

**TV Scoreboard**:
The continuously refreshing, read-only display showing Season Team standings and prizes.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, leaderboard page
